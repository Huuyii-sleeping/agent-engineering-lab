# Document Pipeline

## Workflow

1. Identify the document type, audience, and acceptance criteria.
2. Preserve existing terminology, section hierarchy, and ownership boundaries.
3. Separate facts, decisions, risks, and open questions.
4. Keep operational documents concise and directly actionable.
5. Validate final output for missing sections, stale references, and contradictory statements.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent requirements, dates, owners, or external commitments.
- Keep long reference material in linked sections or appendices when possible.
- Make uncertainty visible instead of converting it into confident prose.
- Avoid decorative language in engineering delivery documents.
